It filters out everything not related to the pattern contained in the filter by setting them to zero and keeps everything that is related to the pattern. So what convolution is doing is it's passing this filter along each point on the original input image and sliding it along at each point. It asks is the pattern here is the pattern here is the pattern here. And so forth. Then it gives us a high number in the positions where the pattern is found and then a small number where the pattern is not found and thus this is your first alternative perspective on convolution. It's just a sliding pattern finder that passes through an entire image. Looking for a particular pattern.
