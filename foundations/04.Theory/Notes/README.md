## The clean-scan edition

Every notebook page appears on its own A4 page, exactly as written — the handwriting is never
retouched, redrawn or reordered. Each photo is only:

- **turned upright** where it was taken sideways, and **levelled** by its small tilt;
- **cleaned**: the bedsheet and table around the notebook are removed and uneven lighting and
  shadows are evened out, so the paper reads white and the ink (including coloured ink) stays crisp;
- **never cropped on the paper** — where a photo also caught part of the neighbouring page, that
  strip is kept so no writing is lost.

Around the pages the PDF adds a cover, a contents page with page numbers, a divider for each part,
a running header (part) and footer (topic), the notebook page number under every page, and
nested PDF bookmarks (part → topic). Some pages were photographed twice in the source; both shots
are kept, in their original order.

## Rebuilding

The edition is generated by [`tools/notes-to-pdf/scan_build.py`](../../../tools/notes-to-pdf/scan_build.py)
from the full-resolution photos (one image per page, in notebook order); the topic index, page
orientation and volume split live in
[`tools/notes-to-pdf/scans/ds-ml-handwritten-notes.json`](../../../tools/notes-to-pdf/scans/ds-ml-handwritten-notes.json).

```bash
pdfimages -j notebook.pdf photos/pg     # or: pdftoppm -jpeg notebook.pdf photos/pg
python tools/notes-to-pdf/scan_build.py photos/
```
